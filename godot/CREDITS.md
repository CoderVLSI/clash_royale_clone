# Third-party assets

3D models from Sketchfab (CC BY 4.0), reduced for mobile with `tools/sketchfab/process.py`:

* **Guard Tower** by Maxim Silkov - https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/guard-tower-d953998e0e2645ce897b1a5286d4e0e6 - used as the princess tower (`assets/models/tower_princess.glb`).
* **Stylized Magic Tower** by Alex.Latigun - https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/stylized-magic-tower-5c98a209e3b54912a87ef1d34f7b4be6 - used as the king tower (`assets/models/tower_king.glb`).

Changes: decimated, rescaled, textures reduced to a small base-colour map (and brightened), metal/normal maps removed, team flag added in code.
